# Kora Dating

Source of truth: **`conception_metier/kora-dating-v1.md`**. This skill carries the
engineering consequences.

Kora's role here is **entremetteur** — matchmaker. It creates matches between
people. The process is deliberately simple, and keeping it simple is a product
decision, not a V1 shortcut.

Nothing exists in the code yet. This is a new bounded context.

## Registration: eight fields, in order

| # | Information | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First name | |
| 2 | Age | Gate: under 18 is refused. See Controls. |
| 3 | Sex | |
| 4 | Profession | |
| 5 | Country and city of residence | A user may live outside Cameroon — ask the country, then the city |
| 6 | Photo | See the photo rule, which must be stated **before** asking |
| 7 | Personal description | Free text. Encourage a clear, relevant description **of the kind of love they are looking for** — this is the primary match criterion |
| 8 | Where are you looking for love? | Asked only once the description is in. Options: your neighbourhood, your city, your country, outside the country. **Several may be chosen**, or "peu importe" |

As everywhere in Kora, the user may give several answers in one message: store
what arrives and ask only for what is missing.

Note that field 5 breaks an assumption the rest of Kora makes. Housing, Food and
Found are Cameroon-local; Dating is not. Location here is country-first, and the
neighbourhood index that serves the other domains does not cover a user in Paris
or Douala-adjacent diaspora. Model residence as country + city, and do not force
it through the Cameroonian neighbourhood resolver.

## The photo rule

Before asking for the photo, Kora states clearly that:

- the photo must not be generated by AI;
- **any photo that is filtered, generated or modified by AI leads to account
  deletion.**

Saying this *before* the upload is the point: a rule announced afterwards is a
punishment, while a rule announced first is a standard people meet. The
consequence is severe, so the warning has to be unmissable and the detection has
to be good enough to justify it — a false deletion is worse than a missed filter.
Decide who reviews a contested deletion before launching.

## Matching

| Priority | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **The two descriptions** |
| 2 | **Location filter** — residence plus the answer to "where are you looking for love?" |

Descriptions first, location as a filter. That ordering is the product: Kora
matches on what someone wrote about the love they want, then restricts by where
they are willing to find it. A location-first implementation with text as a
tiebreak is a different, worse product.

Consequences worth planning for:

- "Peu importe" and multi-select answers mean the location filter is a set, not a
  single value. A user open to "my country" and "outside the country" is not
  asking for a radius.
- Matching on free text is the one place in Kora where a model reads user prose
  to produce an ordering. Keep the result advisory and inspectable: store why two
  profiles were matched, so a bad match can be explained and corrected.
- Reciprocity has to be defined. Does A's location preference have to include B's
  residence, or must both include each other's? The spec does not say; pick one
  and write it down.

## Mandatory controls

Kora must detect:

- inappropriate images;
- inappropriate text;
- **inappropriate ages — under 18**;
- images containing no person;
- very blurry images.

These are not optional quality filters. Three of them protect a person rather
than the product: the age gate, the inappropriate-image check and the
inappropriate-text check. Treat them as a precondition for a profile becoming
visible at all — screen on submission, not on first report.

Where this lives: Trust & Safety is a shared context (see `kora-domain-map`), and
these controls are its largest new responsibility. Dating must not implement its
own parallel screening.

Practical requirements the spec implies:

- **Nothing becomes visible before it is screened.** A profile pending review is
  not discoverable.
- **A refusal must be explainable** to the user, without handing them a recipe for
  passing the filter next time.
- **The under-18 case is not a soft failure.** Do not store the profile and hide
  it; refuse it, and do not invite a retry with a different number.
- **Minimise what is kept.** A face photo and a free-text description are personal
  data under Cameroon's loi n° 2024/017; collect only what the eight fields
  require, keep photos in private encrypted storage, and never put them or the
  description in logs.

## What a match unlocks, and the payment

Settled 11 October. A match does **not** open a conversation inside Kora: it
opens the right to pay **1 000 FCFA** to unlock the other person's contact, the
same unlock model as Housing and Found.

**Unlocking requires a reciprocal match. Consent always precedes payment.** This
is the rule the whole domain's safety rests on — without it, anyone could buy
anyone's number, and the mandatory controls would not compensate. It is also why
revenue here is deliberately lower than a model that sells access on demand.

Kora is a matchmaker, not a chat application: here too its work ends at the
introduction.

## Scope

V1 is registration, the two-level match, the controls, and the unlock above.
Premium features are mentioned as a possibility elsewhere but are **not** part of
this spec — do not build boosts or tiers here.
